# Everyday AI tutorials — 2026-09-23

Eight original practice topics, translated into English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. Drafting and translation used ordinary Chat, not Work. Existing drafts were reused for translation; they are not independent AI accuracy tests.

Topics: document version comparison, notice-to-FAQ, multi-label feedback themes, plain-language instructions, research question planning, user-research interviews, constrained brainstorming, and presentation Q&A rehearsal. These extend the existing tutorials with different outputs and exercises, rather than replacing earlier articles.

All notices, participants, opinions, operating rules, examples and numerical values are fictional. Samples explicitly distinguish editorial demonstrations from actual searches, interviews, AI product execution, or field trials. No new product capabilities, current prices, legal requirements or measured popularity are asserted.

Build with `tools/build_expansion_articles.py`. Verify with `tools/verify_expansion_articles.py`, `tools/verify_site.py` and `tools/verify_ai_discovery.py`. Translation checks cover section/block/table/list structure, source-number retention, evidence identifiers, rendered prompts, canonical URLs and language alternates. Time allocations and multiple-label counts are independently recalculated in Python.

The eight Korean drafts contain 48 sections, 49 copyable text blocks and 26 tables. The brainstorm example allocates 30 minutes for each of four different activities; print counts are 8, 16, 0 and 8. Zero printed handouts does not mean zero paper or no advance preparation. The interview plan totals 15 minutes; presentation practice totals 8 minutes. Neither duration was measured with real participants.
